Everything has a date. What happens next is up to you.

ShelfLife Radar helps independent grocers notice what time is doing to their shelves — and decide, while there is still time, what to do about it.

Open the demo

Why it matters

Nothing on a shelf goes off all at once. It happens quietly — a carton pushed to the back, a date nobody read. ShelfLife Radar is made for the moment before that, when there is still time to discount, move or reorder.

How it helps

  1. i.

    Organise your inventory

    Every product and its date, kept in one place.

  2. ii.

    Notice expiry risks

    Dates are checked against today, so nothing slips quietly past.

  3. iii.

    Decide what needs attention

    What needs you first comes first, with a suggested next step.

Inside the app

Three tabs. One job each.

01 — Shelf

What needs you first, first.

Every batch is sorted by its date, with the next step spelled out — mark it down, move it to the front, or take it off sale. Record a sale in two taps, and see what a markdown leaves you over cost.
